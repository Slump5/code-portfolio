About Royal Window Cleaning

Royal Window Cleaning proudly serves Maple Grove and the Northwest Metro area with professional window cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, and blind repair services.

Our History

Royal Window Cleaning was founded in 2001 by Greg Hoerger. When economic changes required restructuring his blind repair business, Greg began providing professional window cleaning services while completing his bachelor’s degree in Manufacturing Management.

During this time many homeowners encouraged him to continue the service and referred him to friends and neighbors throughout the Northwest Metro area. After graduating, Greg chose to focus fully on growing the window cleaning business.

Today Royal Window Cleaning continues to serve returning customers each season while welcoming new homeowners across the Northwest Metro.

— Greg Hoerger, Owner

Reputation

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: provide high-quality service at a fair price while ensuring 100% customer satisfaction. We aim to build long-term relationships with homeowners by providing dependable home maintenance services year after year.
