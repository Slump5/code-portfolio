Frequently Asked Questions

Why hire a professional window cleaner?

There is definitely an art to cleaning windows perfectly. We have mastered the techniques required to guarantee 100% satisfaction for every customer.

We are always on time, courteous, and professional, so customers continue using our services year after year.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full insurance coverage including general liability and worker’s compensation so our customers have complete peace of mind.

How do you determine the price?

Estimates are always free. In many cases you do not need to be present for us to provide a quote. Pricing is based on the number and type of windows your home has.

Use the guide below to estimate your window count before calling.

How long does window cleaning take?

The average home takes approximately 2–3 hours to complete both interior and exterior window cleaning.

Do I need to be home?

No. Exterior cleaning can be completed without you being home. If interior windows are included we will coordinate access ahead of time.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept venmo, zelle, cash app, and of course cash and checks.

What if it rains?

We can clean windows in light rain. If weather conditions become too severe we will happily reschedule your appointment.

What areas do you serve?

Royal Window Cleaning proudly serves homeowners throughout the Northwest Metro area including Maple Grove, Plymouth, Medina, Golden Valley, Minnetonka, Orono, Wayzata, Champlin, Rogers, Brooklyn Park, Anoka, St. Michael, Dayton, Andover, Minneapolis, Blaine, Coon Rapids, Hopkins, Edina, and St. Louis Park.

Do you clean window screens?

Yes. Screens are removed, washed with low pressure water, dried, and reinstalled once the windows are cleaned.

Can you remove hard water stains?

Yes. We have a process to remove most hard water stains. If stains have etched into the glass we can significantly improve their appearance but minor marks may remain.

How often should windows be cleaned?

Most homeowners have their windows cleaned twice per year, typically in the spring and fall.

Other common times include before selling a home, after pressure washing siding, or before special events.

Do you clean windows in winter?

Yes. Winter window cleaning is available depending on weather conditions. Ask us about winter discounts.
